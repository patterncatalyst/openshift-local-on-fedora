Appendix · Kubernetes patterns on OpenShift

The patterns, worked on OpenShift with our own services.

A chapter per pattern across the six families of cloud-native Kubernetes design (foundational, structural, behavioral, configuration, advanced, and security), each worked on OpenShift with the guide's shipping services instead of a textbook's toy app. Every manifest satisfies restricted-v2, services face the host through Routes, and the operator and image-builder patterns use OperatorHub and the integrated registry.

It's the OpenShift corollary to the minikube Kubernetes-patterns appendix. Every chapter carries a DO280/EX280 exam lens and an Explore with your agent call-out, and a runnable example under examples/appendix-patterns/.

Where this fits in the guide

This appendix is reached from §18 of the main guide. The earlier chapters stood up the cluster and its building blocks: deploying, Routes, Helm, Operators, and the §17 data mesh wired them into one running system. This appendix steps back and names the patterns behind those pieces, one at a time, with the DO280/EX280 exam as a second lens.

The examples run on a pristine CRC cluster; the host prerequisites are the same as §1. This is the OpenShift corollary to the minikube Kubernetes-patterns appendix, which works the same patterns on plain Kubernetes.
